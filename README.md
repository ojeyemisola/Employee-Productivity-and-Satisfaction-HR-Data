## Executive Summary

This project analyzes Human Resources (HR) data to identify the factors influencing employee productivity and job satisfaction using statistical analysis and interactive dashboards.

The HR dataset was imported, cleaned, and prepared for analysis by checking for missing values, inconsistencies, and duplicate records. The cleaned dataset was then used to perform descriptive statistics, data visualization, and multiple regression analysis.

The study focuses on understanding how employee characteristics such as Salary, Age, Gender, and Position influence key organizational performance indicators including Satisfaction Rate, Productivity Rate, Projects Completed, and Performance Evaluation.

The findings provide data-driven insights that can help HR managers improve employee engagement, workforce productivity, and organizational decision-making.

---

## Introduction

Employees are one of an organization's most valuable assets, making it essential to understand the factors that influence their productivity and satisfaction. Human Resource Analytics enables organizations to transform employee data into actionable insights that support strategic planning and better workforce management.

This project applies statistical analysis techniques to explore the relationship between employee demographics, compensation, and job roles with key performance outcomes. The analysis aims to identify patterns that can support evidence-based HR policies and improve organizational performance.

---

## Objectives of the Study

The primary objective of this project is to examine the effect of employee demographic and job-related factors on organizational performance using multiple regression analysis.

Specific objectives include:

- Determine whether Salary, Age, Gender, and Position significantly influence Employee Satisfaction Rate.
- Examine the effect of Salary, Age, Gender, and Position on Employee Productivity Rate.
- Investigate how demographic and job-related variables affect the number of Projects Completed.
- Analyze the influence of Salary, Age, Gender, and Position on Employee Performance Evaluation.

---

## Research Questions

1. Does Salary, Age, Gender, and Position significantly influence Employee Satisfaction Rate?

2. How do Salary, Age, Gender, and Position affect Employee Productivity Rate?

3. What relationship exists between Salary, Age, Gender, Position, and the number of Projects Completed?

4. To what extent do Salary, Age, Gender, and Position influence Employee Performance Evaluation?
